1. **Xu huong da dang hoa**: Thu nhap ngoai lai TB tang tu 14.79% (2015) den 18.15% (2024).

3. **Rui ro trading**: 7 NH co bien dong trading income cao (top 25%) — nguon thu khong ben vung.

4. **Bancassurance**: 15 NH co thu bao hiem >5% tong thu dich vu — chien luoc bancassurance dang phat huy hieu qua.

5. **Digital proxy**: 13 NH co thu thanh toan vuot median (45.9%) — dau hieu chuyen doi so manh.
