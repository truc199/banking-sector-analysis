## 6. Key Insights

1. **NPL toan nganh**: NPL trung binh tang tu 1.74% (2020) len 2.87% (2024).

2. **Muc do du phong**: 18 NH co ty le bao phu < 100% (thieu du phong), 3 NH co bao phu >= 150% (du phong day).

3. **Chi phi tin dung**: Nhom chi phi tin dung cao (top 25%, >1.28%) gom 7 NH — dang tra gia cho chat luong tai san kem.

4. **Ap luc du phong**: 5 NH co du phong chiem >50% PPOP — loi nhuan that bi bao mon nghiem trong boi no xau.

5. **Canh bao som**: 7 NH co no can chu y (Nhom 2) o muc cao (>1.73%), co nguy co chuyen thanh no xau trong tuong lai.
