## 5. Key Insights

1. **NIM toan nganh**: NIM trung binh tu 2.51% (2015) den 2.79% (2024). Dinh NIM dat 3.23% vao nam 2022.

2. **Spread (Yield - Cost)**: Spread trung binh mo rong tu 3.26pp (2015) den 3.54pp (2024). NH co loi the canh tranh khi duy tri spread rong.

3. **Phan hoa NIM**: Chenh lech NIM giua NH tot nhat va kem nhat (2022--2024) la 5.49pp. Do lech chuan = 1.06pp.

4. **NIM vs Chi phi huy dong**: Tuong quan r = -0.418. NH co chi phi huy dong thap co xu huong NIM cao hon.

5. **NIM Compression**: 18/27 NH bi thu hep NIM (2022->2024), 3 NH mo rong. Da so NH dang chiu ap luc thu hep bien lai.
