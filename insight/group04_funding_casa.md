## 4. Key Insights

1. **CASA toan nganh**: CASA trung binh giam tu 16.41% (2015) den 15.72% (2024).

2. **Phan hoa CASA**: Chenh lech CASA giua NH tot nhat va kem nhat la 33.14pp. NH CASA cao co loi the chi phi von re, gop phan cai thien NIM.

3. **Phu thuoc von thi truong**: 7 NH co ty le wholesale funding >15% — rui ro thanh khoan va chi phi von cao hon.

4. **On dinh tien gui**: NH co ty le TG ca nhan cao (>68.2%) co co so KH ben vung hon. Nhom nay gom 13 NH.

5. **CASA va NIM (tham chieu cheo Nhom 2)**: NH co CASA cao thuong co chi phi huy dong thap, giup duy tri NIM tot. Day la loi the canh tranh cot loi trong moi truong lai suat giam.
